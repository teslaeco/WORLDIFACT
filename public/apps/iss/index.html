FORGE / LEARNING TO REPAIR

Small actions.
Great responsibility.

Getting started: collect the orange tool bag and put on the EVA suit near the airlock. EVA route selects the next required stop. At the airlock press ACT/E to begin the exterior transition.

Moving in 3D

On a phone, use the large joystick on the left. A small movement gives a slower speed. UP and DOWN change altitude. On a computer: WASD, Space and Ctrl/Q. Shift increases EVA speed.

Camera

Drag the scene with your right thumb while moving the character with your left. Pinch or use the wheel to zoom. In EVA the camera is clamped to a safe orbit around the astronaut. Reset view returns to a safe entry view without erasing repair progress.

Tools

Open Tool bag, select a tool and press ACT within range of the workstation. For bolts, hold the button and release in the green zone. Keyboard controls also include 0–8 and E.

Progress and parts

Repairs consume parts from the bag. Refill supplies where you collected it. Save downloads a progress file; Load restores it at a safe position inside the station.

Model and exercises

The exterior map uses the NASA / Visualization Technology Applications and Development ISS model. This historical configuration includes Pirs but does not include Nauka, Prichal or iROSA.

The interior, astronaut, tools and orange workstations are original models. The tether illustrates a connection to the airlock without simulating its tension. EVA drifting shown by the character is visual/game motion, not a real orbital dynamics simulator.

Project idea: explore station maintenance and discuss protecting its heritage. These are training-game interactions, not ISS operating procedures. Game results do not determine whether preserving the entire ISS in orbit is technically feasible.
