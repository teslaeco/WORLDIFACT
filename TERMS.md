# Preview terms — release draft

Updated 15 September 2026. The matching in-app notes are at `/terms`.

WORLDIFACT provides an early procedural world editor and a manufacturing workbench. Astra returns a structured scene specification, not an arbitrary reconstructed production mesh. GAME exports contain geometry and materials; they do not include controller code or production certification.

Use only references and files you have permission to use. The source repository's MIT licence does not grant rights to imported photographs, likenesses, trademarks, characters or third-party assets. Linked projects retain their own licences and terms.

All MAKE outputs remain candidates until geometry, dimensions, minimum walls, assembly, color and the selected supplier's requirements have been reviewed. Calculator observations and user-entered assumptions are not binding offers. WORLDIFACT currently places no manufacturing orders and collects no payments. These notes create no manufacturing contract or promise of physical safety.

LIVE preview access requires a code, a valid expiry and a global request allowance. Failures, refusals, client cancellations and timeouts may consume an attempt. Important work should be exported and retained by the user.

The project is maintained by Terraforming Planet. Confirm the operator's legal identity/private contact and final release terms before public LIVE activation. These are factual preview notes for review, not a claim that legal review has been completed.
